About Monochrome

Individuality. Confidence. Timeless beauty.

Who We Are

Monochrome is a modelling agency founded by Blessed Khoza in 2026. The agency was created around the visual language of black-and-white photography and a belief in authentic individual expression.

Our purpose is to connect models with opportunities while encouraging professionalism, confidence and creative growth.

Our Philosophy

Black and white represent simplicity, elegance and timelessness. Our visual identity removes unnecessary distraction and places attention on the person in front of the camera.

We welcome individuals from different backgrounds and encourage each model to bring their own identity, story and presence to the work.

Our Vision

To build a respected modelling community where individuality is valued, diversity is visible and emerging talent has room to develop.
