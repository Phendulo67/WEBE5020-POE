# WEBE5020-POE
A website for a modelling agency which has a black and white aesthetic

BRIEF HISTORY Monochrome is a Modeling agency that was founded by Blessed Khoza in 2026, the founder of the organization had a goal of uniting people of different races and so from the comfort of his home in Wasbank he started an Instagram page which he used to showcase the beauty of people as a whole and he achieved this by exclusively posting images in black and white, which in theory was meant to remove any bias regarding skin tone or complexion

THE MISSION Monochrome has a clear mission, which is to unite all South Africans and help to eliminate any prejudice or negative stigmas surrounding skin color, showing people that there is beauty in everyone regardless of color, uniting everyone under the banner of black and white THE VISION To emotionally empower all South Africans no matter their skin tone or background TARGET AUDIENCE Monochrome’s target audience is not limited to a single type of person but opens itself to all people who want to showcase the natural beauty of the South African people to the rest of the world CONTACTS AND SOCIAL MEDIA Instagram Facebook Twitter/X WEBSITE GOALS The website’s goals are to establish a stronger online presence, provide valuable information, generate more website traffic, and make it easier for more people to contact the modeling agency. The organization has an average of 100 models in every province and 3 branches in each, including schools. The website also aims to employ the youth to assist with camera work and photoshoots ,this aims to improve youth unemployment and empower them for a prosperous future Technical Requirements & Tools HTML-In order to build the structure of the website and it’s various pages HTML will be used to make the basic layout and architecture of the website. CSS- To style and decorate the website and it’s pages, CSS will be used for styling the various fonts and images, backgrounds, etc. Visual Studio Code- This is a software that will allow us to program the languages used (HTML & CSS), as it has built in compilers for the languages and is easily accessible. Unsplash- This software was used to access free to use images for the website
